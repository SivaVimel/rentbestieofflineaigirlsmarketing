RentBestie

Someone to talk to,
entirely on your phone.

RentBestie is a companion app with a language model inside it. Swipe through thousands of fictional characters, open a profile, and start talking. Every reply is written on your device — no account, no servers, no connection needed.

It works in airplane mode. The AI is not a service RentBestie calls. It is a model that ships inside the app and runs on your phone's own processor. There is no networking code in the app at all, so nothing you type can go anywhere.

Swipe, then talk

A deck of 2,470 companions, each with a face, a home town, a job, a sense of humour and a set of things she is into. Swipe right on the ones you like, left on the ones you don't. Anyone you keep turns up under Interested, waiting for you to say hi.

Changed your mind? Nothing is lost. Everyone you passed on sits in a Not interested list you can take them back from at any time.

She stays in character

Each companion's profile is what the model is briefed on — so what you read on her profile is what she knows about herself. Ask her about her weekend and she will answer with hers. She remembers the last few messages, so a conversation holds together.

She is also small enough to run on a phone, which means she will sometimes be wrong, repeat herself or make something up. The app says so plainly rather than pretending otherwise.

Nothing leaves your device

  • No account, no sign-in, no email
  • No analytics, no advertising, no identifiers
  • No server that receives anything, because there is no server
  • No camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or location access
  • Your chats live in the app's own storage and go when you delete it

The character photographs are bundled inside the app and are never written to your photo library, so nothing from RentBestie shows up in your gallery.

Everyone here is fictional

Every companion is invented. The photographs are AI-generated and depict no real person; the names, ages, home towns and life stories are made up, and the places they come from do not exist. RentBestie is a companionship app, not a service for meeting real people. It is intended for adults.

Made to be pleasant

Dark by default, with a light theme if you prefer. Full Dynamic Type support and VoiceOver labels throughout. No pop-ups, no streaks, no energy meters, no upsell.

What you need

iPhone iOS 16.4 or later ~1.6 GB free space No internet required

The app is large because the model and every photograph ship inside it. That is the trade: a bigger download once, and then it simply works, offline, forever, without an account.
